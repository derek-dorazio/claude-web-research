# Skill: Roll-Up Thesis

Construct the investment thesis for consolidating a fragmented services vertical via a platform acquisition plus add-ons.

## When to Use

Apply after establishing that a vertical is fragmented (see `industry-fragmentation`). This skill answers the four search-fund thesis questions — **why this industry, why now, what are the value-creation levers, and how do we exit** — and stress-tests the thesis against risks.

## Instructions

1. **Frame the consolidation logic**: Why does combining small operators create value here? Look for: subscale G&A and back-office that can be centralized, purchasing/payor leverage, technology/marketing that independents can't afford, referral-network density, and labor pooling.

2. **Why now**: Identify timing catalysts — demographics (e.g., aging population driving home health/hospice demand), reimbursement or regulatory shifts, owner-retirement / succession waves (silver tsunami of baby-boomer owners), or technology inflection. Use WebSearch for recent trend reporting and cite it.

3. **Value-creation levers** — quantify where possible:
   - **Multiple arbitrage**: entry multiples for sub-$3M EBITDA targets (typically 3–5x) vs. platform exit multiples at scale (often 7–12x). This is the central return driver — show the spread.
   - **Margin expansion**: shared services, G&A leverage, procurement.
   - **Organic growth**: cross-sell, geographic density, de-novo expansion.
   - **Add-on cadence**: realistic number of tuck-ins per year given deal flow from `deal-sourcing`.

4. **Identify acquirers / exit pathways**: Who buys consolidated platforms in this vertical? Use the SEC EDGAR MCP and Yahoo Finance MCP to find public strategics/consolidators and recent platform sales; WebSearch for PE platforms active in the space. This validates the exit and frames the multiple-arbitrage target.

5. **Stress-test risks**: reimbursement/regulatory exposure, customer or payor concentration, labor availability and wage inflation, integration/diligence risk, key-person dependence at targets, and whether PE has already compressed entry multiples.

6. **State the thesis crisply**: a falsifiable one-paragraph thesis plus the conditions under which it breaks.
