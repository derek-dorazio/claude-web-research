## 1. Recommendation & Summary
We recommend approving, subject to conditions, the acquisition of Access Healthcare, Inc. — a $6.0M-revenue, ~$1.1M-EBITDA skilled home-health operator in East Providence, RI — at $5.5M (5.0x) as the **platform** for a Rhode Island home-health roll-up. The thesis is multiple arbitrage plus shared-services margin: buy sub-scale operators at ~4x, integrate onto Access's back-office, and build toward an 8x+ exit. The single biggest risk is reimbursement/payer-mix exposure, which we gate behind a quality-of-earnings and regulatory review.

## 2. The Opportunity
Access is owner-operated, profitable, and for sale via a regional healthcare M&A advisor with a transition-willing owner. Rhode Island home health is fragmented (185 NPPES providers, no dominant local platform), giving a long add-on runway from a single metro base.
